# Privacy Policy — Mera Paisa Tracker

Last updated: 20 Nov 2026

Mera Paisa Tracker is an **unofficial guide**. It is not a government app and is not affiliated with the Government of India or Government of Gujarat.

The app is distributed in India only.

## What the app itself collects
Nothing. The app itself does not collect, store, or share:
- Name, phone number, email, address
- Aadhaar number, bank account, OTP, or any financial details
- Location, contacts, SMS, photos

## What stays on your phone
Your tracker checklist ticks (e.g. "e-KYC done") are saved only on your phone using local storage. They are never sent to any server — there is no server. Ticks are kept until you uninstall the app; uninstalling deletes them. If the app crashes, a short error log is also saved on your phone only (to help fix the bug); it is never sent anywhere and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

## Spoken questions
The app reads the 6 eligibility questions and their answer options aloud in Gujarati using the phone's own text-to-speech. Nothing is recorded and no voice leaves your phone. Answers are tapped on screen; there is no microphone input.

## Links
Tapping "સત્તાવાર પોર્ટલ ખોલો" opens the government website in your browser (e.g. pmkisan.gov.in, digitalgujarat.gov.in, ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in). Those sites have their own privacy policies.

## Ads & third parties
The app shows ads served by Google AdMob, and earns revenue from these ads: one small banner, one full-screen ad at most once every 2 minutes, native ads inside the schemes list (marked "Ad"), and one app-open ad when the app starts. AdMob may collect your device advertising ID and approximate location to show ads; it does not receive your name, Aadhaar, bank or OTP details from us. You can reset your advertising ID in your phone's Settings > Google > Ads. In regions where advertising consent is required, the app shows Google's consent form first and serves non-personalised ads until you give consent. Watching a rewarded ad is your choice — if you watch one fully, the banner is paused for 3 days. No analytics SDK, no loan offers, no lead selling.

## Children
The app is intended for adults / household heads. It collects no data from anyone, including children.
